## Bibliography and source scope

### NORTON

Norton, R. L. (2020). *Design of Machinery* (6th ed.). McGraw Hill. ISBN 978-1-260-11331-0.

**Supporting scope:** Mechanism kinematics, linkages, slider-cranks, gear trains, motion analysis, and machine dynamics.

**Publisher/standards page:** https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/design-of-machinery-norton.html

### RAO

Rao, S. S. (2022). *Mechanical Vibrations* (6th ed.). Pearson. eText ISBN 978-0-13-751528-8.

**Supporting scope:** Free, damped, and forced vibration; resonance; vibration isolation; and SDOF/MDOF modeling.

**Publisher/standards page:** https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/mechanical-vibrations/P200000003425

### SHIGLEY

## Source-boundary rule

- **FE-Handbook-supported** — directly supported by the FE Reference Handbook location recorded in the ledger.
- **Externally supported** — required by the FE Mechanical specification but not fully developed in the Handbook.
- **Guide synthesis** — explanatory linkage, worked examples, limiting-case checks, and design context created for the supplemental guide.

A `split_required: true` atom remains marked because the concept has mixed-source scope. It is considered reconciled when its Handbook basis, external support, and guide-synthesis boundary are explicit.
