**Publisher/standards page:** https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/engineering-mechanics-statics-dynamics/P200000003247/9780137514724

## Source-boundary rule

- **FE-Handbook-supported** — the underlying equation, table, definition, or discipline model is directly supported by the FE Reference Handbook location recorded in the ledger.
- **Externally supported** — the Other Disciplines specification requires application/integration knowledge that is not fully developed in the Handbook.
- **Guide synthesis** — cross-domain classification, sequencing, checks, and exam strategy created for this supplemental guide.

A `split_required: true` atom remains marked because its scope intentionally crosses source boundaries. It is considered reconciled when the Handbook basis, external support, and guide-synthesis boundary are explicit.
