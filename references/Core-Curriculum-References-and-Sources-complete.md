# Core Curriculum — References and Source Reconciliation

## Purpose

This register supplies the unified reference map for **Layer 1 (01-01 through 01-41)** and **Layer 2 (02-01 through 02-70)**. It complements the Layer 3 discipline-track reference registers.

The FE Reference Handbook remains the exam-facing authority wherever the ledger marks a concept `in_handbook`. External references support material that is omitted, only summarized, or developed beyond the Handbook. Chapter-level mappings are intentionally broader than the individual `split_required` records so that prerequisite material that is `not_in_handbook` also has a source family.

## Bibliography and source scope

### NCEES_HB — NCEES FE Reference Handbook 10.6

National Council of Examiners for Engineering and Surveying (NCEES). *FE Reference Handbook*, Version 10.6.

**Supporting scope:** Exam-facing formula/table/reference authority.

### NIST_SP811 — NIST SP 811

National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). *Guide for the Use of the International System of Units (SI)*, Special Publication 811.

**Supporting scope:** SI usage, unit style, conversions, significant figures, and logarithmic quantities.

### NIST_SP330 — NIST SP 330

National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). *The International System of Units (SI)*, Special Publication 330.

**Supporting scope:** SI base/derived-unit definitions and conventions.

### OPENSTAX_STATS2E — OpenStax Introductory Statistics 2e

OpenStax. *Introductory Statistics 2e*.

**Supporting scope:** Probability, distributions, sampling, inference, regression, and hypothesis testing.

### OPENSTAX_CA2E — OpenStax College Algebra 2e

OpenStax. *College Algebra 2e*.

**Supporting scope:** Algebra, equations, functions, polynomials, systems, logarithms, and sequences.

### OPENSTAX_PRECALC2E — OpenStax Precalculus 2e

OpenStax. *Precalculus 2e*.

### OPENSTAX_CALC2 — OpenStax Calculus Volume 2

OpenStax. *Calculus Volume 2*.

## Provenance rule

Use the Handbook citation from `meta/ledger.yaml` for Handbook-supported content. Use the chapter-level source family above for external instructional support. Where the guide combines several sources into a workflow, label the result as **guide synthesis based on** those sources rather than attributing the workflow to one publication.
