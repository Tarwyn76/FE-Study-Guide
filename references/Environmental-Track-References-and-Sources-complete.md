## Bibliography and source scope

### MIHELCIC

Mihelcic, J. R., & Zimmerman, J. B. (2021). *Environmental Engineering: Fundamentals, Sustainability, Design* (3rd ed.). Wiley. ISBN 978-1-119-60445-7.

**Supporting scope:** Environmental measurements, chemistry, physical processes, biology, risk, water quantity/quality, water treatment, wastewater/stormwater, solid waste, and air quality.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://www.wiley-vch.de/en/areas-interest/engineering/environmental-engineering-978-1-119-60445-7

### METCALF

Metcalf & Eddy/AECOM, Tchobanoglous, G., Stensel, H. D., Tsuchihashi, R., & Burton, F. L. (2014). *Wastewater Engineering: Treatment and Resource Recovery* (5th ed.). McGraw-Hill. ISBN 978-0-07-340118-8.

**Supporting scope:** Wastewater characteristics, physical/chemical treatment, activated sludge, solids recycle, biosolids, residuals, and resource recovery.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://www.mheducation.com/highered/product/wastewater-engineering-treatment-and-resource-recovery-metcalf-and-eddy.html

### SAWYER

Sawyer, C. N., McCarty, P. L., & Parkin, G. F. (2003). *Chemistry for Environmental Engineering and Science* (5th ed.). McGraw-Hill. ISBN 978-0-07-119888-2.

**Supporting scope:** Environmental stoichiometry, acid-base chemistry, oxidation-reduction, solubility/precipitation, partitioning, and reaction chemistry.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://books.google.com/books?id=d0yTDAEACAAJ

### EPA_RAGS

U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. (1989). *Risk Assessment Guidance for Superfund, Volume I: Human Health Evaluation Manual (Part A)*. U.S. EPA.

**Supporting scope:** Baseline human-health risk assessment: data evaluation, exposure assessment, toxicity assessment, and risk characterization.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://www.epa.gov/risk/risk-assessment-guidance-superfund-rags-part

U.S. Environmental Protection Agency. (2011). *Exposure Factors Handbook: 2011 Edition* (EPA/600/R-09/052F), with subsequent chapter updates where applicable. U.S. EPA.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://www.epa.gov/expobox/exposure-factors-handbook-2011-edition

**Supporting scope:** PPE hazard assessment, eye/face, respiratory, head, foot, hand, electrical, and fall protection requirements.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910subparti

### NIOSH_NOISE

**Supporting scope:** Occupational-noise exposure and hearing-loss-prevention criteria.

**Authoritative/publisher page:** https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/docs/98-126/

## Source-boundary rule

- **FE-Handbook-supported** — directly supported by the FE Reference Handbook location recorded in the ledger.
- **Externally supported** — required by the FE Environmental specification but not fully developed in the Handbook.
- **Guide synthesis** — explanatory linkage, worked examples, verification checks, and study structure created for the supplemental guide.

A `split_required: true` atom is not an unresolved defect by itself. It is resolved when the Handbook basis, external support, and guide-synthesis boundary are explicit.
