## Basis and constraints

This map preserves the project structure already established in the guide:

- Layer 2 contains **70 chapters** across six tiers.
- Tier 2C contains **19 chapters**.
- Tier 2D contains **18 chapters**.
- Existing ledger seed chapters are preserved exactly at **02-22**, **02-23**, and **02-24**.
- The source material fixes the six tier subjects but does not supply chapter-by-chapter titles for the full 70-chapter layer.
- Therefore, the chapter titles and the chapter-count split for 2A, 2B, 2E, and 2F below are **new project-planning decisions**, designed to preserve the fixed numbering and align the shared-core content with the FE Reference Handbook sections and FE specification topics.

## Build-order intent

The intended authoring order is sequential unless a concept-level prerequisite forces a later chapter to move earlier. The concept ledger remains the authority for dependency validation. Chapter-map order does **not** override the forward-reference linter.

The three existing Tier 2C seed entries retain their current locations:

- **02-22** — Constructing a Free-Body Diagram
- **02-23** — Equilibrium Equations for a 2D Concurrent Force System
- **02-24** — Method of Joints for Planar Trusses

Before each chapter is drafted, its concept atoms should be added or expanded in the ledger with explicit prerequisites, term ownership, Handbook status, specification mappings, and figure ownership. Planned chapter titles alone do not count as concept registration.

## Source boundary

The following are source-established project facts: six Layer 2 tiers and their subjects, 70 total Layer 2 chapters, 19 chapters in Tier 2C, 18 chapters in Tier 2D, and the existing 02-22 through 02-24 ledger seeds. The remaining titles and tier-count allocations are the newly established planning map, not claims that those titles already existed in the prior ledger or Handbook.
