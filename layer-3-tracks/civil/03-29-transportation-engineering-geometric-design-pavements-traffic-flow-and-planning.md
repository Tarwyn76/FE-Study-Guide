## 29.1 Stopping sight distance and roadway geometry

Stopping sight distance combines distance traveled during perception-reaction with braking distance. Use the Handbook's unit-specific relation.

\[SSD=\text{perception-reaction distance}+\text{braking distance}\]

## 29.5 Traffic flow variables and fundamental relationships
