## Source policy

These tier reviews are cumulative checkpoint forms assembled from the existing chapter review banks. They therefore test material actually taught by the guide and preserve a source-chapter/concept trail. They are not intended to be a separate copyrighted/public question bank.

The available Library snapshot of Chapter 01-21 contains its review-question section but no answer key. Its three Tier 1C items were therefore derived directly from the chapter's integration-by-parts, trigonometric-integral, and p-test teaching sections.
